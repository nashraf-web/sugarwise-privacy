Privacy Policy for SugarWise

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

SugarWise ("the app") is developed by OuzzTech. We designed SugarWise so your health information never leaves your phone.

Data We Collect

None on any server. SugarWise works 100% offline. The following data is stored only in the app's private database on your device:

  • Blood sugar (glucose) readings you log
  • Blood pressure and weight entries you log
  • Medication logs you log
  • App preferences (units, reminder times, theme, language)

We have no servers, no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics SDKs. We cannot see, access, or share your health data — because we never receive it.

Health Data

SugarWise handles health information. This data is processed only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party. You can delete all app data via Android Settings → Apps → SugarWise → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

Purchases

SugarWise offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not see, collect, or store your payment details.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): only for reminders you schedule, processed locally.
  • Vibrate: only for haptic feedback.
  • Exact alarms / boot: only for scheduled reminders.
  • Billing: only to unlock Pro via Google Play.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

Advertising and Tracking

SugarWise contains no ads and no third-party trackers or analytics.

Children

SugarWise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Medical Disclaimer

SugarWise is a personal health tracker, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult your doctor about your health.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
